Frequently Asked Questions

What type of business processes can be automated?

Repetitive, rule-based and data-driven processes are often strong candidates, including approvals, reporting, notifications, document handling and system-to-system workflows.

Do we need to replace our existing ERP?

Not necessarily. Many automation solutions can work with existing ERP, databases, APIs and business applications.

Can you automate Microsoft 365 workflows?

Yes. Depending on the requirement, Power Automate can be used with Microsoft 365 applications and supported business systems.

Can you build an AI chatbot for our business?

Yes. AI assistants can be designed for internal knowledge, customer support or specific business workflows, depending on the use case and available data.
