Frequently Asked Questions

What is business process consulting?

It involves reviewing how business activities are performed, identifying inefficiencies and recommending improved processes, controls and supporting technology.

Can you prepare BRD and functional documents?

Yes. Documentation can include business requirements, functional requirements, process flows, SOPs, roles and reporting requirements.

Can you help us select an ERP system?

Yes. ERP advisory can include requirement assessment, gap analysis, evaluation criteria and implementation planning.

Can consulting be provided before software development?

Yes. In many cases, completing business analysis first helps reduce development ambiguity and improves project outcomes.
